# Image generation prompt record

All seven assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow for a Glarivo B2B blog article.

## Shared visual direction

- Use case: product-mockup
- Asset type: Glarivo B2B blog hero or body image
- Style: photorealistic premium commercial glassware photography
- Setting: deep navy stone bar counter with a softly blurred modern hospitality background
- Lighting: warm amber rim light with cool navy ambient fill
- Materials: realistic clear optical glass with believable reflections and proportions
- Constraints: unbranded; no text, logos, watermarks, people, hands, bottles, packaging or unrelated drinkware
- Avoid: surreal shapes, colored glass, impossible refraction and decorative details that obscure the silhouette

## Asset-specific requests

- `hero-six-cocktail-glass-types.webp`: exactly six fully visible and clearly separated silhouettes in a horizontal lineup—rocks, highball, coupe, Nick & Nora, martini and shot—with generous negative space for a wide website crop.
- `rocks-glass.webp`: one straight low-profile rocks glass with a substantial base, one large clear ice cube and a small amount of neutral amber beverage.
- `highball-glass.webp`: one tall straight-sided highball with a stable base, clear ice, transparent lightly sparkling beverage and one plain citrus peel.
- `coupe-glass.webp`: one shallow rounded coupe with a gently inward rim, slender stem, broad foot and pale clear strained beverage.
- `nick-and-nora-glass.webp`: one compact tulip-shaped Nick & Nora with a small rounded bowl, slender stem, compact foot and pale clear stirred beverage.
- `martini-glass.webp`: one classic symmetrical V-shaped martini glass with a slender stem, broad foot, clear chilled beverage and one small olive.
- `shot-glass.webp`: one empty compact straight-sided shot glass with thick walls and a substantial stable base.
