# Image prompts — How to Read Glassware Spec Sheets for Wholesale

Mode: built-in ImageGen

Use-case taxonomy: `product-mockup`

## Shared visual direction

Professional B2B editorial product photography for a wholesale glassware specification guide. Clear, unbranded glassware in a neutral product-development or quality-inspection setting; realistic transparent glass, accurate tool geometry and restrained warm-gray, charcoal, kraft and clear-glass palette. Soft diffused studio lighting, documentary precision, no luxury styling. No faces, brands, logos, readable documents, real product codes, legible measurements, certification marks, captions, typography or watermark. Avoid colored beverages, unsafe handling, broken glass, distorted tools, impossible reflections and any implication that an illustrated value is a verified Glarivo specification.
