# Image generation prompt record

All five assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow for a Glarivo B2B wine-glass buying guide.

## Shared visual direction

- Use case: product-mockup
- Style: photorealistic premium commercial glassware and technical documentation photography
- Setting: modern hotel, sample-review room or clean logistics area using a deep navy, neutral gray and kraft-paper palette
- Lighting: soft daylight with restrained warm highlights
- Materials: realistic clear optical glass with believable proportions and reflections
- Constraints: unbranded; no promotional copy, logos, watermarks or unsupported performance/certification cues
- Avoid: ornate cut-crystal styling, impossible refraction, unreadable pseudo-branding and exaggerated luxury effects

## Asset-specific requests

- `hero-wine-glass-profiles.webp`: exactly five fully visible and separated empty silhouettes—tall tapered red-wine, wide balloon, medium all-purpose, champagne tulip and compact dessert glass—arranged in a horizontal hotel-restaurant lineup.
- `capacity-confirmation.webp`: one all-purpose wine glass beside one water-filled graduated cylinder in a sample-review room; the process should be understandable without relying on readable text or calibrated markings.
- `dimension-inspection.webp`: one wine glass with a digital caliper across the outside rim, a height ruler and a blank specification sheet; measurement geometry should be believable, with no readable values.
- `construction-detail-inspection.webp`: one full clear wine glass beside an inspection magnifier emphasizing rim, bowl-to-stem joint, stem and foot geometry without implying composition or certification.
- `protective-carton-review.webp`: exactly six clear wine glasses individually protected by corrugated dividers and molded-pulp supports inside an open master carton.
