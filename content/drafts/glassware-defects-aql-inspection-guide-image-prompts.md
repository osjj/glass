# Image prompts — Glassware Defects & AQL Inspection Guide

Mode: built-in ImageGen

Use-case taxonomy: `product-mockup`

## Shared visual direction

Professional B2B editorial product photography for a wholesale glassware quality-control guide. Clear, unbranded glassware; neutral warm-gray quality-inspection environment; realistic transparent glass, reflections and proportions; restrained beige, charcoal and clear-glass palette; soft diffused studio or inspection lighting; polished but documentary rather than luxurious. No people’s faces, no logos, no brand names, no legible documents, no certification marks, no numerical readings, no captions, no typography and no watermark. Avoid unsafe handling, broken shards exposed on the work surface, dramatic factory sparks, colored beverages, distorted glass geometry and impossible reflections. Images are illustrative process references only and must not imply a particular factory, result, standard, certification, sample size or acceptance threshold.
